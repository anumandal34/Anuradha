A little about me

Hello! I'm Anuradha Mandal, an IT professional from Janakpur, Nepal. I completed my 10+2 at Shree Rajeshwar Nidhi Secondary School and then earned my Bachelor's degree in BSc.CSIT (Computer Science and Information Technology) at Rajarshi Janak University Campus, Janakpurdham-08.

My studies gave me a solid foundation in programming, databases and problem solving. I work with HTML and CSS to build web pages, Java and Python to write logic and applications, and SQL to store and query data. I'm especially interested in data analysis: turning raw data into clear insights that help people make better decisions.

I'm a curious and disciplined learner who enjoys breaking a big problem into small steps and solving it patiently. I like learning new languages and tools, and I try to write code that is clean, simple and easy to understand. I work well independently and in a team, and I'm always open to feedback.

My goal is to grow into a skilled developer and data analyst, and to contribute to real projects in the IT industry. I'm currently looking for opportunities where I can apply what I've learned and keep improving every day.
